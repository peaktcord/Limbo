Oblivion controls
=================

WASD / arrows    Move / navigate (hold to keep walking)
Space / E / F / Enter / left mouse  Attack / select / confirm
Tab / C / I      Character sheet / inventory
Escape / Backspace / P / right mouse  Menu / back
1 / H            Quick health
2 / M / R        Quick magicka
Q / X / middle mouse  Toggle weapon / spell
F11              Toggle phone/boundary camera and wide/centered camera (with modern 8-way movement)
Alt+Enter        Toggle fullscreen

Top-row 0 and 3-9 and the numeric keypad send the original phone digit codes
used by the in-game Controls screen. Top-row 1 and 2 remain the convenient
quick-item aliases.

Confirm also skips the opening story crawl, which the original could only leave
by waiting for it to scroll to the end.

Confirm automatically follows the highlighted or primary choice on phone-style
Yes/No and OK prompts; Escape, Backspace, P, or right mouse selects Back/No.
In wide mode, a confirm that opens dialogue must be released before a fresh
confirm can dismiss it, replacing the original one-second lockout.
